Adventure Seed

Somewhere past the last star, something still wears the crown.

How to play

Move with the arrow keys or W A S D; Q E Z C move diagonally. Walk into a foe to attack it and into a townsperson to trade. Click a foe to target it, click a distant tile to walk there, or click yourself to use what you are standing on.

1 to 4 use abilities. F fires a ranged weapon or a charged tool at your target. X explores indoors and gathers loot outdoors. R rests. Period waits. Enter uses stairs, den entrances and your ship. T lists places to walk to. V boards or leaves your vehicle. G opens the star map. M hides the minimap. I opens the pack, P the character sheet.

Each world has an outdoor map and two or three dens to go down into. Kill the alpha at the bottom of a den to clear it. Stand on the landing pad and open the star map to jump to a linked star. Danger rises toward the centre, where the Hollow King waits.
